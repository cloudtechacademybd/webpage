Bank & card reconciliation

We match every transaction in your books to your bank, credit card and loan statements, so the balance in your software is the balance in the bank.

Monthly

What's included

  • Reconciliation of all bank, credit card and loan accounts
  • Opening and closing balances tied to each statement
  • Investigation of unmatched or duplicate entries
  • A list of outstanding cheques and deposits in transit

What we need from you

  • Monthly statements for every account
  • Access to your accounting software
  • Answers to any questions on unclear items

You receive

  • A reconciliation report for each account
  • A short note of any items needing your attention

Transaction categorisation

Every transaction is coded to the right account in your chart of accounts, using the same rules each month so your reports can be compared over time.

Weekly or monthly

What's included

  • Coding of income, expenses, transfers and loan payments
  • Bank rules set up for recurring transactions
  • Correct handling of sales tax, VAT or GST where it applies
  • Separation of owner and business transactions

What we need from you

  • Receipts or notes for unusual transactions
  • Your preferred chart of accounts, if you have one

You receive

  • Fully categorised books, ready for reporting
  • A list of transactions we couldn't identify

Statement extraction

When your bank feed is missing or incomplete, we turn PDF statements into clean files you can import straight into your accounting software.

As needed

What's included

  • Extraction of every transaction from PDF or scanned statements
  • Import-ready CSV files for QuickBooks Online or Xero
  • Running balance checked against the statement totals
  • Support for PayPal and other payment platform statements

What we need from you

  • The statements, as PDFs or clear scans
  • Which software the files will be imported into

You receive

  • One CSV file per statement, ready to import
  • Confirmation that each file balances to its statement

Books review & clean-up

If your books are behind or you're not sure they're right, we review them account by account and fix what's wrong before year-end or tax time.

One-time project

What's included

  • Review of every balance sheet account
  • Search for duplicates, miscoding and missing periods
  • Correcting entries with a clear explanation for each
  • Catch-up bookkeeping for months that were never recorded

What we need from you

  • Statements for the full period under review
  • Any prior-year financial statements or tax returns

You receive

  • A review report listing every issue found and fixed
  • Clean, reconciled books ready for your accountant

Monthly financial reports

After each month is closed, you get your key reports and a plain-language summary of what changed and why.

Monthly

What's included

  • Profit & loss statement
  • Balance sheet
  • Comparison with the previous month
  • A short written summary of notable changes

What we need from you

  • Nothing extra once reconciliation and categorisation are in place

You receive

  • Reports as PDF and Excel files
  • A clear list of any open questions

Document organisation

We sort your invoices, receipts and statements into a clear folder structure with consistent names, and tell you which periods are missing.

At onboarding, then monthly

What's included

  • Files renamed by vendor, number, date and amount
  • Folders organised by document type and date
  • Duplicate documents identified and flagged
  • A gap report showing any missing statement months

What we need from you

  • Your documents, in any format — a shared folder or ZIP is fine

You receive

  • An organised document folder
  • An Excel inventory and missing-period report
